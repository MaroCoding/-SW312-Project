# City Marathon Management System

**Course:** C-SW312: Introduction to Software Engineering, Fall 2026
**Institution:** Egypt University of Informatics, Faculty of Computing & Information Sciences
**Current phase:** Deliverable #1 (Chapters 1 & 2: requirements and elicitation)

## Overview
A system to manage a city marathon end to end: event setup, runner registration
and payment, medical clearance, race-day timing and safety, anti-cheating,
family live tracking, and charity fundraising.

## Stakeholders / User Roles
Admin, Runners, Medical Reviewers, Medical Team, Volunteers, Timing Team,
Families, Sweep Bus Drivers, Donors, plus the automated Race System.

## Key Functional Areas
- **Event management:** events, categories, waves, capacity, cutoffs, registration phases and pricing
- **Registration:** accounts, lottery when demand exceeds capacity, payment (local/international), bib collection slots
- **Medical:** certificate upload and review (signed within 6 months), health declarations with restricted access, emergency alerts and response tracking
- **Timing and results:** chip data, mat readings (offline-safe), gun/chip time, cutoff detection, published results
- **Anti-cheating:** missed or unrealistic mat times flagged, runner notified and able to dispute
- **Safety:** pace-drop detection, temperature alerts, sweep bus dispatch
- **Fundraising:** charities, runner fundraising pages, donations
- **Tracking:** live tracking and finish notifications for families
